# DeployHub Pricing Plans

## Free Tier
- 1 project
- 100 deployments per month
- Community support only
- 512MB RAM per deployment
- No custom domains

## Pro Plan ($29/month)
- 10 projects
- Unlimited deployments
- Email support (24h response SLA)
- 4GB RAM per deployment
- Custom domains included
- SSL certificates auto-provisioned

## Team Plan ($99/month)
- 50 projects
- Unlimited deployments
- Priority support (4h response SLA)
- 16GB RAM per deployment
- Team roles and permissions
- Audit logs
- Custom domains included

## Enterprise Plan
- Unlimited projects
- Dedicated infrastructure
- 24/7 phone support
- Custom RAM allocation
- SSO and SAML integration
- On-premise deployment option
- Custom SLA negotiation
- Contact sales for pricing
